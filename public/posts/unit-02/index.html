The nature of information problems

In this unit almost everything new other then the conversion of Binary to Decimal, Decimal to Binary and Hexadecimal. First thing we studied in the class about our Eyes ,parts of Eyes, In our eyes image is inverted and real but we sit it normal. Images storage mainly depends on the pixels. Pixels depends on intensity level 0 to 255(0 = Black ,255 =White and between them Grey colour).If the images are coloured then pixels have 3 times of the normal memory.

We played with the images blurring the image, Horizontally ,Vertically (Edge -sharp).Median filtering by rotating images,Shifting and Reflection of image finding the column and row number. These are the types of Image Transformation. In our lower class we studied properties we used them for the transformation of the images .Associative Transformation[(T3.T2)T1=T3(T2.T1)],Identity Transformation [A`=A],Inverse Transformation [T2.T1(A)=A] and Idenpotence Transformation [T*(T*(A))=T(A)]In this i faced many challenges but interested in solving problems, starting i didn’t understand anything ,after teaching in class and solving lab tasks i understand the questions.

Drawing the Graphs are very tricky. Optimization this is process of finding the possible solution among available alternatives. In here also many peoples go with the greedy approach(Big number or Larger number).We solved many problems in class by greedy approach and Dynamic programming, Train graphs, Knapsack problem.
